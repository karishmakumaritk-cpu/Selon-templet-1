ESTABLISHED 2016

Our Story

Lumière Beauty Studio was founded with a singular, clear vision: to create an elevated sanctuary where personal beauty is celebrated, nurtured, and redefined without the rush of commercial salons.

Over the past decade, we have grown into a distinguished collective of master hair colorists, certified skincare specialists, and visionary bridal artists. We believe in providing an oasis of calm where every guest receives undivided attention and bespoke care.

From the moment you step through our doors, your experience is tailored to your lifestyle, face shape, skin cadence, and unique aesthetic ambitions.

THE FOUNDATION

Our Philosophy

“Beauty should never be standard — it should be an authentic reflection of who you are.”

We reject cookie-cutter trends in favor of timeless technique and personalized harmony. Our goal is never to change who you are, but to accentuate your natural radiance and empower your inner confidence.

THE STANDARD

Our Approach

“Precision in technique, warmth in hospitality, purity in ingredients.”

Every service starts with an open dialogue. We combine continuous international training with clean, cruelty-free formulations to ensure your hair and skin remain healthy, resilient, and luminous long after you leave.
